Why Is Water Important?

Water is one of the most important natural resources on Earth. People, animals and plants need water to survive.

Water pollution happens when harmful materials enter rivers, lakes, oceans and other water sources.

WaterWatch creates awareness about clean water and encourages everyone to protect our water resources.
