Causes of Water Pollution

Understanding the problem is the first step.

Plastic Waste 🥤

Plastic bottles, bags and other plastic waste can enter rivers and oceans and harm aquatic life.

Industrial Waste 🏭

Some industrial activities can release harmful substances into nearby water sources.

Sewage Waste 🚰

Untreated sewage can contaminate rivers, lakes and other sources of water.

Chemicals 🧪

Harmful chemicals from different activities can pollute water and affect living organisms.
